Navidrome

Plays music from your Navidrome (or another Subsonic) server on the phone's loudspeaker. On the phone: Menu, CFW Tools, Navidrome, to browse artists, albums and playlists.

Only http:// works (the phone has no TLS), so use the server's address in your LAN. The password is not stored on the phone, only a salted MD5 token as Subsonic uses it. Navidrome converts the music to MP3 for the phone, which needs its ffmpeg (the official Docker image has it).
