Speaker / reverse intercom

Plays sound from this browser or a PC on the phone's loudspeaker. The phone UI keeps running. While it is on, anyone with the token can play sounds on the phone.

Play your PC's sound on the phone (Linux)

tools/snom-speaker.sh in the repo adds a sound output called "snom D375" to PulseAudio or PipeWire and streams everything played on it to the phone:

tools/snom-speaker.sh PHONE_IP

It asks for the panel password and starts the speaker here if it is off. Pick "snom D375" as the output in your sound settings, or run with -d to make it the default. tools/snom-speaker.sh -f song.mp3 plays one file (needs ffmpeg).
